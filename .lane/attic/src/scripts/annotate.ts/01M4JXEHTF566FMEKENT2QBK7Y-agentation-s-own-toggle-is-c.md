Agentation's own toggle is Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+F; Cmd+Shift+A forwards to it. A modal <dialog> makes the page inert, so the toolbar is re-rendered with portalContainer=<the modal>, which Agentation shows as a manual popover in the top layer. Chrome may reserve Cmd+Shift+A (tab search) in some builds.
